# CommCTV - Child Safety & CSAM Prevention Policy

**Last Updated:** August 27, 2026

CommCTV is strictly committed to maintaining a safe environment and enforcing zero tolerance against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

---

## 1. Zero Tolerance Policy
CommCTV strictly prohibits any content, communication, or media that depicts, promotes, or facilitates child sexual abuse or exploitation. Any such content will be removed immediately, and the associated user account will be permanently banned.

---

## 2. Reporting Mechanism
Users can report any child safety concerns or policy violations:
- **In-App Reporting:** Tap the options menu on any post or profile to report content immediately.
- **Direct Contact:** Email our designated child safety compliance officer at `gdpeterson@urbansyncinn.com`.

---

## 3. Moderation & Law Enforcement Reporting
- All reported content is reviewed within 24 hours.
- Confirmed CSAM/CSAE violations are reported directly to NCMEC (National Center for Missing & Exploited Children) and appropriate regional law enforcement authorities.
- CommCTV complies with all applicable local, national, and international child safety laws.
